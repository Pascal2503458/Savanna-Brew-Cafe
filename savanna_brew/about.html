About Savanna Brew Café

Our story, our passion and our love for Kenyan coffee.

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café was established in 2019 by Amina Otieno, whose love for coffee began while growing up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri.

Inspired by the richness of Kenyan coffee, Amina created a welcoming café where people could enjoy quality coffee, fresh pastries and meaningful connections.

Today, we continue supporting local coffee farmers, using eco-friendly packaging and hosting monthly community events.

Our Mission

To serve excellent Kenyan coffee while creating a welcoming environment that brings people together and supports local communities.

Our Vision

To become a trusted café brand known for quality, sustainability and outstanding hospitality.

Our Core Values

Quality

We carefully select quality Kenyan coffee beans and prepare every order with care.

Community

We support local farmers and create a welcoming space for our customers.

Sustainability

We promote responsible sourcing and environmentally friendly packaging.
